# Nandi: The Sacred Protector 🐂✨

On Valentine's Day, Manu sat down in her quiet corner with just a sketching pencil and a blank page in her sketchbook. She said:

> 🗣️ **Manu said:** "Nandi is the protector of the temple, and he loves wearing bells so Shiva knows when he is happy!"

With that beautiful thought in mind, she began sketching one of her most detailed and sophisticated pieces yet — Nandi, the sacred bull.

## The Details in the Sketch

It is absolutely amazing to see how much focus and patience went into this drawing. Manu paid close attention to several intricate details:

- **The Decorated Horns:** She drew bands and rings wrapped around Nandi's horns, giving him a regal and festive look.
- **The Face Chain:** A delicate chain of tiny beads draping elegantly across Nandi's face.
- **The Bells (Ghungroo):** Along the strap on Nandi's neck, she drew small bells, ending in a large hanging bell under the chin.
- **The Eyes and Shading:** Manu spent extra time shading the snout and around the eyes to give Nandi a gentle, peaceful expression.

She even signed the artwork with a stylized signature "Qi" and wrote the date "14-Feb-26" at the top right, marking the day she completed this masterpiece!

## What We Love About It

This sketch shows how much Manu's pencil control and attention to detail have grown. The shading is delicate, and the proportions are incredibly well thought out for a young artist. It looks like a classic sketch you would find in an art museum!
