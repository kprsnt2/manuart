# The Magic of Speed Bumps 🚗💨

A few days ago, while dropping Manu off at school, we went over a speed bump on the road (which she calls a \"speed break\"). After we crossed it, she looked at me and shared a very interesting theory:

> 🗣️ **Manu said:** "If we go fast this will become big, and if we go slow it will become small!"

At first, I smiled and thought it was just a silly kid's idea. How could a solid bump on the road change its size?

## The Great Realization 💡

But on the drive back home, I started thinking about what she said. Suddenly, it hit me — she is a genius, and I was the one who didn't understand!

If you drive fast over a speed bump without braking, the car jolts violently. It feels like a massive obstacle on the road (a \"big\" bump). But if you slow down and cross it gently, you barely feel it (a \"small\" bump). 

In physics terms, she was describing the relationship between speed, force, and impact. It was a perfectly accurate observation of how our perception of the bump changes based on velocity!

I was absolutely amazed. It's a creative way of looking at the world that I never would have thought of on my own.
