Manu made a pencil sketch of Nandi (the sacred bull) in her sketchbook.
She completed it on Valentine's Day (14-Feb-2026).
She signed it with a cute signature that looks like "Qi" or "Qi." and wrote the date "14-Feb-26".

She added beautiful details to Nandi:
- Beautifully decorated horns with rings/bands.
- A necklace chain with tiny circular beads draped across the face.
- A strap with bells (ghungroo) along the neck and a main hanging bell.
- She focused a lot on shading the eyes and the snout.

We think it looks incredibly artistic, almost like a professional sketch!
